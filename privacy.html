Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 October 2026. Sira CV is made by KRM Studio ("we"). This policy explains what the app keeps, what leaves your phone and why.

Stays on your phone

Your CVs, cover letters, CV and LinkedIn checks, job applications, interview answers, goals and settings are stored on your device. CV or LinkedIn PDF/DOCX files you open are read on the device. Photos you use in the LinkedIn photo maker are processed on the device and are not uploaded. Translations run on the device (Google ML Kit downloads the language model once).

What we collect, only when you choose

  • Account (optional): your name and email address, or your Google account name, email and profile picture if you sign in with Google. Used only to sign you in.
  • Backup (optional, off by default): if you turn on backup, your CVs and cover letters are saved to our database so you can restore them on a new phone.
  • Purchases: an anonymous purchase ID and Google Play purchase records, processed by RevenueCat, so we can unlock Premium.
  • Ads: Google AdMob uses your device's advertising ID to show ads to users who have not removed them. Where the law requires it, you are asked for consent first; if you decline, only non-personalised ads are shown.

Reminders and tips are local notifications scheduled on your phone. We do not use analytics, we do not read your contacts, location, microphone or photo library, and we do not sell your data.

Who processes it

  • Supabase (sign-in, optional backup and app content updates)
  • Google Sign-In (if you choose it)
  • RevenueCat and Google Play (purchases and subscriptions)
  • Google AdMob (ads, with your consent where required)

Data sent over the network is encrypted in transit (HTTPS).

Children

Sira CV is a career app for people aged 16 and over and is not directed at children.

Keeping and deleting your data

Delete everything on the phone in Profile, Delete all my data. Delete your account and its backup in Profile, Delete account, or see how to delete your account. Purchase records may be kept as long as the law requires. Subscriptions must be cancelled in Google Play.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR) you can ask to access, correct or delete your data. Contact us and we will reply within 30 days.
