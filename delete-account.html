Delete your Sira CV account

You can delete your account and everything we keep on our servers at any time.

In the app (fastest)

  1. Open Sira CV
  2. Tap Profile
  3. Tap Delete account and confirm

This removes your account and your backed-up CVs and cover letters right away. To also clear the phone, tap Delete all my data. Purchase records may be kept as long as the law requires.

By email

If you can no longer open the app, email supportkrmstudio@gmail.com from the address linked to your account with the subject "Delete my account". We delete the data within 30 days and confirm by email.

Subscriptions

Deleting your account does not cancel a subscription. Cancel it in Google Play: Account, Payments and subscriptions, Subscriptions.
